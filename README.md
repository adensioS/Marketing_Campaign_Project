📌 **Project Overview**

This project applies **unsupervised machine learning** techniques to segment customers from a retail marketing dataset.
The goal is to **uncover distinct customer groups, understand their demographic and behavioral characteristics**, and translate those insights into **actionable marketing strategies**.

The analysis focuses on customer profiling, not just model output—bridging the gap between data science and business decision-making.

### 🎯 Business Objectives
- Identify natural customer segments using clustering techniques
- Profile each cluster based on demographic and household attributes
- Translate clusters into real-world marketing strategies
- Support targeted campaigns, pricing strategies, and product positioning
